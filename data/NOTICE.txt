Lookup data provenance

Personal names: Faker 40.44.0, en_US and de_DE person providers.
Streets and fictional city/postcode pairs: generated once with Faker 40.44.0
using seed 20261103, then stored in words.csv and places.csv.
City/postcode combinations are fictional and may not be geographically valid.

Faker is used only to prepare the CSV dictionaries. The deployed application
has no Faker dependency. All runtime contact generation runs in PostgreSQL.
